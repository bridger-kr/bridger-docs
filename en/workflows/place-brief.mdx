<Warning>This is a workflow contract, not a live result. It does not guarantee nationwide charging coverage, real-time data, currently available chargers or distance-ranked results.</Warning>

## Users and inputs

Product teams and automation partners repeatedly checking Korean regional environmental information are the intended users.
Inputs include a Korean region name or coordinates, weather issue date/time, forecast target time and a verified air-monitoring station name.
`resolve_location` can normalize location into weather-grid/administrative codes, but does not automatically establish an air-monitoring station.
Invalid coordinates and ambiguous names require clarification.

This contract requests all four components: location, weather, air quality and charging stations.
Acceptable adoption means an operator confirms using a briefing that explicitly preserves differing times, geographic scope and omissions.
Recommendations, safety guarantees, reservations, medical judgments and route optimization are out of scope.
Raw addresses, coordinates, queries and keys are excluded from optional analytics.

## Existing guides and tools

1. Reuse [AI connection](/en/guides/claude) and [first tool](/en/getting-started/first-tool) authentication/activation.
2. Check `resolve_location` coverage and ask the user to disambiguate locations.
3. Use `weather/village-forecast`, `getVillageForecast` in the [weather catalog](/en/presets/weather) and the existing weather widget. Distinguish forecasts from observations.
4. Read station and measurement time with `environment/air-quality-realtime`, `getAirQualityRealtimeByStation` in the [environment catalog](/en/presets/environment).
5. `transportation/ev-charging`, `getEvCharging` in the [transportation catalog](/en/presets/transportation) covers **Suwon**. Other regions are `unsupported_region`.
6. Preserve reference times and missing-data reasons for human review. Fetching results and adopting them are separate actions.

Station coordinates/addresses do not make the provider query a distance-ranked or charger-availability search.
Determining nearby stations needs separately verified coordinates/distance computation; that was not executed for this example.

## Outcome contract

This page and synthetic fixtures describe a proposed normalized envelope, not changes to current MCP responses or connectors.
Each requested component includes `source`, `asOf` and `unavailable`.

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `success` | All four components have usable rows with known reference times. Not `adopted` until a person confirms use |
| `partial` | Some usable rows, but another component is empty/unavailable. Show reasons such as outside Suwon, missing measurements or stale forecast |
| `empty` | No usable rows and at least one valid empty response; preserve any other errors |
| `error` | Every component unavailable due to keys, input, timeout, provider error or similar failure |

Unsupported regions and stale data are not successful rows.
Unknown reference times remain `missing_asof`; never insert the current time to disguise them.
Partial results are not complete/first successes. Review again after input/key correction or approved retries.
